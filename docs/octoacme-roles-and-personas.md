# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Subject Matter Expert (SME)

### Role Summary
Subject Matter Experts provide deep technical or domain expertise to validate requirements, guide implementation decisions, and ensure solutions align with best practices.

### Responsibilities
- Validate requirements for technical and domain accuracy
- Provide technical guidance during planning and execution
- Identify domain-specific constraints, risks, and assumptions
- Review proposed solutions for feasibility and best-practice alignment

### Goals
- Ensure solutions are technically and domain-appropriate
- Reduce rework by clarifying requirements early
- Improve decision quality with expert input

### Typical Communication
- Collaborates with Developers on technical feasibility and implementation trade-offs
- Works with Product Managers to validate requirement intent and scope
- Advises Project Managers on domain risks that may impact timeline and delivery

---

## Compliance & Governance Specialist

### Role Summary
Compliance & Governance Specialists ensure project work adheres to regulatory, security, and organizational policy requirements throughout planning and execution.

### Responsibilities
- Review requirements and deliverables for compliance alignment
- Identify governance, policy, and regulatory gaps early
- Provide remediation guidance for compliance issues
- Partner with teams to embed compliance checks into delivery workflows

### Goals
- Minimize regulatory and policy risk
- Ensure secure and compliant delivery outcomes
- Prevent late-stage compliance blockers

### Typical Communication
- Works with Developers to confirm controls, security requirements, and implementation safeguards
- Partners with Product Managers to align scope and priorities with governance constraints
- Coordinates with Project Managers to schedule compliance checkpoints and approvals

---

## Cross-Functional Liaison

### Role Summary
Cross-Functional Liaisons bridge communication between teams, coordinate dependencies, and ensure critical information flows across siloed functions.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate cross-team dependencies and handoffs
- Surface and resolve communication gaps between teams
- Ensure alignment on sequencing, ownership, and timelines
- Attend key meetings to represent impacted partner teams

### Goals
- Improve cross-team alignment and execution efficiency
- Reduce delays caused by dependency mismanagement
- Maintain clear accountability across teams

### Typical Communication
- Syncs with Developers to communicate technical dependencies and integration needs
- Connects Product Managers with partner teams on scope impacts and trade-offs
- Supports Project Managers by tracking cross-functional blockers and coordination actions

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts gather, clarify, and document requirements, translating business needs into technical specifications that delivery teams can execute.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business, functional, and non-functional requirements
- Translate business needs into clear technical specifications
- Clarify acceptance criteria and assumptions with stakeholders
- Collaborate with SMEs to refine technical feasibility and detail

### Goals
- Produce clear, actionable requirements
- Reduce ambiguity between business and technical teams
- Improve planning accuracy and delivery predictability

### Typical Communication
- Works with Developers to clarify detailed requirements and expected behavior
- Partners with Product Managers to ensure requirements align with product outcomes
- Collaborates with Project Managers to align requirements readiness with delivery plans

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define test strategy, establish quality metrics, and validate deliverables against acceptance criteria.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain project-level testing strategy
- Establish quality metrics and validation criteria
- Ensure test coverage aligns with requirements and risk
- Validate that deliverables meet acceptance criteria before release

### Goals
- Improve product quality and release confidence
- Detect defects early and reduce production issues
- Maintain consistent quality standards across deliverables

### Typical Communication
- Collaborates with Developers on testability, defect resolution, and quality gates
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and release quality expectations
- Coordinates with Project Managers on test milestones, readiness, and quality risk reporting

---

## Project Planning Interaction Pattern
- During Project Planning: Product Owner and Project Manager work with Business Analyst to gather requirements → Business Analyst collaborates with SMEs to refine technical feasibility → Compliance Specialist reviews for governance gaps → QA Lead defines test strategy based on requirements

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
