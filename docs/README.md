# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to the OctoAcme project management documentation hub. This guide is the starting point for understanding how teams plan, deliver, release, and improve work across the full project lifecycle.

OctoAcme follows a five-phase lifecycle: **Initiation, Planning, Execution, Release, and Close & Retrospective**. The approach is grounded in customer-first principles, iterative delivery, and clear ownership between Project Managers (PMs), Product Managers (PdMs), developers, QA, and stakeholders. Governance is intentionally lightweight but explicit, with decision gates, key artifacts, and role accountability at each phase.

Execution emphasizes strong team rhythm and quality discipline. Teams run daily standups and weekly syncs, track work through a visible board, and ship small pull requests (ideally 400 lines or fewer) with clear acceptance criteria. Automated linting, testing, and security scanning in CI support quality gates, while unit, integration, and smoke tests protect reliability before release.

Risk management and communication are continuous throughout delivery. Teams maintain a Risk Register, escalate blockers through defined paths, and communicate status to stakeholders on a regular cadence. Release and incident practices include rollback playbooks and post-incident learning, and structured retrospectives feed actionable improvements back into future planning and execution.
